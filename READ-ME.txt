Put your pictures in this folder.

Then open content.js and write the picture's name where it says  image: ""
For example:   image: "images/frankenshark.jpg"

Tips
- Use simple names: lowercase, no spaces (frankenshark.jpg, not "Frankenshark FINAL (2).JPG").
- Film stills look best wide, about 1600 pixels across.
- Keep each picture under 500 KB so the site stays fast.

What is already here
- film-NAME.jpg        the thumbnail for that film (from your old portfolio)
- film-NAME-still.jpg  the bigger picture on that film's own page
- mani-portrait.jpg    your photo on the About page
- mani-at-work.jpg     your photo on the Contact page
